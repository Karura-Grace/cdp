Running the staff conversation

One hour with a department group of five or more, no line manager in the room. Your job is to listen. The 41 people who answered want to know they were heard.

The hour in four steps

1

Open and set the ground rules

Time: 10 minutes

Explain that answers were anonymous and that no group under five people is shown.

You might say: “Nothing you say today will be traced back to you.”

2

Share what the survey found

Time: 15 minutes

Show the four voice scores below zero, starting with innovation climate at −29.

You might say: “These are your numbers. We want to understand what sits behind them.”

3

Listen

Time: 25 minutes

Ask about the top two comment themes: recognition and decisions. Write down what people say, not who said it.

You might say: “What would have to change for this score to go up?”

4

Agree one change

Time: 10 minutes

Ask the group to pick the one change they want first. Note it in their words.

You might say: “If we could fix one thing this quarter, what should it be?”

Please avoid
  • Do not name or guess who gave an answer.
  • Do not discuss any single leader's 360 result.
  • Do not promise a change the executive team has not agreed.
