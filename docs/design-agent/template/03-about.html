About this report

The Culture Dynamics Profile shows how Zendaro Group works today, how its people want it to work, and where the culture supports or holds back the strategy. It reads culture through four orientations: Engage, Innovate, Perform and Govern.

Each chapter leads with a finding, shows the evidence behind it and ends with what it means for Zendaro Group.
