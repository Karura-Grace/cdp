D

Working through the report

1Session 1

Executive read out

Who

The five executives with the Culture Practitioner.

Purpose

Agree what the results say before anyone else sees them, and face the 7.23 point Engage split together.

Agenda
  1. 1The five priorities and the evidence behind each
  2. 2Why staff see Engage differently from executives
  3. 3What the executive team will say to staff, and when
Outputs

An agreed message to staff and a named owner for each priority.

2Session 2

Department heads working session

Who

The heads of Finance, Operations, People and Culture, Sales and Technology.

Purpose

Read each department's profile against the organisation and plan the boundary sessions.

Agenda
  1. 1Each department's lead orientation and its difference from the organisation
  2. 2Which differences to protect and which to close
  3. 3The quick wins each head owns in the first 30 days
Outputs

A 30 day plan for each department and dates for the boundary sessions.

3Session 3

Staff conversations

Who

Staff in department groups of 5 or more, led by a trained facilitator, with no line manager in the room.

Purpose

Test the findings with the people who gave them, and hear what the numbers do not say.

Agenda
  1. 1What the results say about this department
  2. 2The four voice scores below zero: what sits behind them
  3. 3One change the team wants first
Outputs

Themes fed back to the executive team without names.

Non negotiables for every session
  • Results are shared as they are. No number is softened or left out.
  • No individual leader's 360 result is shown or discussed in any session.
  • No group of fewer than 5 people is discussed on its own, and what staff say is fed back without names.
  • Every session ends with an owner and a date for each agreed action.
