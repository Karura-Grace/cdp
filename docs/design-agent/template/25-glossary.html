Glossary

Adaptive capacity
How well teams adjust their work when conditions change. One of the three readiness measures, on a 1 to 5 scale.
Band
The word the platform gives a score so it can be read without a reference table, such as Moderate or Significant.
Congruence
Whether practice, strategy and leadership point to the same orientation. Fragmented means they point in different directions.
Culture Practitioner
The PAC professional who runs the assessment, reads the results with the client and guides the change.
Culture Risk Index
One reading, from 0 to 100, of how exposed a planned change is to cultural friction. Lower is better.
Culture strength
How clearly one orientation stands out from the others. A diffuse culture has no clear leader.
Detractor, passive, promoter
The three groups each voice score sorts people into, from least to most favourable.
Digital readiness
How prepared people feel to adopt new tools and ways of working. A readiness measure on a 1 to 5 scale.
Dimension
One of six areas of organisational life the profile examines: Cultural DNA, Leadership Blueprint, People Architecture, Cohesion Drivers, Strategic Compass and Victory Conditions.
Dominant orientation
The orientation with the highest Now score. The secondary orientation comes next.
Employee voice score
A reading from −100 to +100 on one of eight topics, such as leadership or communication. Above zero is Positive.
Engage
The orientation that puts people, relationships and belonging first.
Even share
25 points for each orientation: the profile a culture would show if none stood out. Drawn as a dashed line on profile charts.
Executives and staff
The two groups compared in chapter 06. Each needs at least 5 answers to be shown.
Flag
A platform marker that a result needs attention. Always printed with the Watch icon and a word.
Gap
Preferred minus Now for an orientation or dimension. A positive gap means people want more of it.
Govern
The orientation that puts structure, control and reliable process first.
Hit, Partial hit, Miss
The three verdicts on a strategy check: the culture supports the strategy, supports it in part, or works against it.
Innovate
The orientation that puts new ideas, experiment and change first.
Leadership 360
Feedback on each leader from the leader and from colleagues. Results are private to each leader; this report shows only the collective pattern.
Leadership 360 coverage
The share of headcount led by leaders with a valid Leadership 360 result. Below 60 percent, the readiness score is marked provisional.

CGlossary, continued

Leadership alignment
How closely leaders' behaviour matches the culture the strategy needs, on a 1 to 5 scale.
Lens
The angle a question takes: Practice (how work gets done), Strategy (what the organisation aims for) or Leadership (how leaders behave).
Now and Preferred
Now is the culture people experience today; Preferred is the culture they want. Each splits 100 points across the four orientations.
Orientation
One of the four cultural directions the profile measures: Engage, Innovate, Perform and Govern.
Perception gap
The difference between how executives and staff score the same orientation.
Perform
The orientation that puts results, targets and competition first.
Psychological safety
Whether people feel safe to speak up, admit mistakes and disagree. A readiness measure on a 1 to 5 scale.
Readiness
How prepared the organisation is to absorb change, read from digital readiness, psychological safety and adaptive capacity.
Reporting threshold
The smallest group shown on its own: 5 people. Smaller groups count in the totals but are never shown separately.
Shadow culture
A flag raised when executives and staff describe the organisation so differently that they are, in effect, describing two cultures.
Spread
How much people disagree about a score. A wide spread means experience differs across the organisation.
Strategy check
One of four tests of whether the culture supports the strategy: strategy, leadership behaviour, systems and business model.
Subculture
A department whose profile differs clearly from the organisation, sometimes led by a different orientation.
Tension
A pair of orientations pulling against each other, such as Engage and Perform, given a band.
Voice index
The overall employee voice reading across all eight topics.
Watch and Significant
Classes for the size of a gap: Watch from 4 to 8 points, Significant above 8. Watch gaps are worth monitoring; significant gaps call for action.
