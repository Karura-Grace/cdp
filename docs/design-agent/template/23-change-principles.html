12.4Change principles and non negotiables

Six principles guide every action in this plan, and four rules hold however strong the pressure to move fast.

1

Listen before launching

Executives and staff sit 7.23 points apart on Engage. Each initiative starts from what staff said, not from what leaders assume.

2

Keep what works in Perform

Perform leads at 29.64 and carries delivery. People want less of it (−10.15), so ease how people are managed, not the standards customers receive.

3

Grow Innovate where it already lives

Technology scores 35.0 on Innovate, the highest of any department. Spread its methods before buying new ones.

4

Give the reason for every decision

Decisions draw 26 percent of comments. Every change states why, who decided and what happens next.

5

One scorecard, local actions

Five departments show four different lead orientations. Heads adapt the actions; the scorecard stays the same for all.

6

Recognise the behaviour you want

Recognition is the top comment theme at 29 percent. Thank Engage and Innovate behaviour in public, every month.

Non negotiables
  • No leader is named or ranked from the 360 results. Each leader's result is private and used for development only.
  • No result is shown for a group of fewer than 5 people.
  • The executive team answers the listening round in writing before any programme launches.
  • Delivery standards stay in force while Perform eases. The change is in how people are managed, not in what customers receive.
