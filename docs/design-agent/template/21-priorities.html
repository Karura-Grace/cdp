Five priorities, in order. Start with the 7.23 point split on Engage between executives and staff.

1Priority 1 · EngageOwner: CEO and executive teamTiming: Days 1 to 30

Close the split between how executives and staff see the organisation

Finding

Executives and staff describe two different organisations. The split is widest on Engage, where the platform raises a shadow culture flag.

Consequence

Programmes designed at the top will read as out of touch, and more communication will deepen cynicism before any content lands.

Action

Run an unfiltered listening round in all five departments before any programme is announced, and have the executive team answer what it hears in writing.

Signal to watch  Executive and staff Engage scores move closer at the next pulse, and the shadow culture flag clears.

Consequence

20 of 41 people are detractors on development. Without visible paths to grow, the shift to Innovate has few people ready to carry it.

Action

Publish a growth path for every role family and protect learning time. Explain each major decision and its reasons on the floor within a week, since decisions draw 26 percent of comments.

Signal to watch  Detractors on development fall below promoters (now 20 against 9), and recognition stops leading the comment themes.
