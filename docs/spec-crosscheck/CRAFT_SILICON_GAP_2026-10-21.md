## 5. Inconsistencies in the proposal

1. **Fees.** The line items add up to $58,575 (2,550 + 2,375 + 5,000 + 6,900 + 2,450 + 8,600 + 4,400 + 300 + 5,000 + 21,000), not the stated $69,030. VAT ($11,044.80) and the payment schedule are computed on $69,030.
2. **Re-assessment.** Table 4 says the 12 month re-assessment is an optional add on not included in the priced fees, yet line 10 charges it ($5,000).
3. **Employees invited.** About 300 (section 4.2), about 350 (section 4.7 and Table 4), about 650 (Table 2).
4. **Leader 360 scope.** "Leader 360 for all the leaders" (Figure 1) against eight senior leaders everywhere else.
5. **Typo.** Table 1, Day Two: "he Culture Champion role".
