# Culture Dynamics Profile: rehearsal guide

**Date:** 20 October 2026 (third edition)
**Scope:** A step by step guide for PAC managers to test the whole platform themselves, from a new client with nothing in it to the next cycle, as every role, before a real client does
**Status:** Updated for the engagement Overview and tabs, the client logo, the full length report and the Leadership 360 preview. Every step in Part A is run end to end against the platform with exactly the data in this guide before each edition. Part B follows the two demo clients already in the platform.
**Live site:** https://culture.pac.africa

## 1. Start here

### Why this guide exists

This guide lets a PAC manager test the platform end to end without help. It puts the work in the order it happens, says who does each step and where to click, gives every value to type, and says what you should see. Part A builds a new client from nothing (about two hours, spread over a day because the survey window must close). Part B walks two prepared clients in about 25 minutes. Note anything that does not match this guide, with the step number, and send it to the platform team.

One thing to know first: the setup form exists only while a client is in the Setup stage. A client that has already been scored shows its results instead.

### The order of the whole product

## What changed on 18 October

Read this first if you used the earlier edition. The steps below still work, with these differences.

- **Sections E, F and G need two entries.** Open **Perspectives** (a tab on the engagement). The Client Admin or the consultant saves an Executive entry and an HR entry for each of E, F and G. The consultant compares them side by side, notes where they differ, and only then agrees the shared section. Agreeing without both entries is refused.
- **Order of work.** B, C and D are agreed before E, F and G. B to G are agreed before Section I.
- **Executives confirm.** Once a section is agreed, an Executive can tick it as confirmed on the read only **Setup review** page.
- **Facilitator.** On each workshop record you pick the facilitator from the consultants assigned to the engagement. Participants must be people already loaded in the organisation whose grade is marked executive. Typed names no longer count.
- **Scorecard.** A new **Scorecard** tab holds objectives and KPIs. One button imports them from Sections C and D. Once the scorecard has rows, the launch check L4 reads it.
- **Section I is longer.** It now has cohorts, the anonymity commitment, the reminder plan, the demographic attributes people will be asked about (each with a purpose), and a tick to say the staff message states purpose, use of results, anonymity and next steps.
- **Launch checks.** There are now more than L1 to L8: a DPIA check, the cohort sizes, the review period overlap, a public holidays warning and the staff message checks. L7 and L8 now report real differences. The consultant types the public holidays under the calendar on the launch panel.
- **Staff message approval.** Launch is blocked until the owner approves the two staff message drafts on the **Staff messages** page (Super Admin menu). During a rehearsal you will see check I5 fail until that is done. This is intended.
- **Privacy tab.** It holds the DPIA record, the processing register, sub processors, breach incidents with the 72 hour clock, data subject requests, retention and the destruction certificate, demographic attributes and coach assignments.
- **Survey changes.** After consent, respondents may see an **About you** screen with closed lists and a Prefer not to say answer. Every survey screen now links to the rights page where people can ask for access, correction, objection or erasure.
- **Raters.** A **Raters** tab lets a leader propose raters and a line manager or Client Admin approve them (three to eight peers). The consultant sets the rater dates per leader.
- **After launch.** Holidays and the review period can still be corrected, and the staff message wording can be edited, both audited. Anything that changes scoring needs a new assessment version, and that is refused once anyone has answered.

## What changed on 20 October (Journey 2)

- **Two more launch checks.** EXG: at least one grade must be marked executive. EXT: an Executive must have ticked each of sections A to D on the **Setup review** page. Both block launch. Editing a section clears its tick, so it has to be ticked again.
- **Currency.** Each client has one currency, set on the **Organisations** screen. Money KPIs imported from Section C take it. Without it they are skipped, and the reason is shown.
- **Section A across clients.** The Super Admin menu has a **Section A** page listing name, sector, headcount and countries for every client. It can be edited until the client's latest engagement launches.
- **B6 pre-filled.** Section B starts with a business model suggested from the sector and maturity stage. This is a draft table for faculty review; change it freely.
- **The survey.** Each statement shows its orientation name. The counter reads "x of 36 scenario sets". The arrow keys change the points by 1 (or 5 with Shift). A practice scenario comes first and is never saved. Answers are saved on the server after every screen, encrypted, so a respondent can resume on another device. On the practice screen a respondent may tick "submit what I have if the survey closes". If ticked, the fully answered scenarios are submitted when the data is frozen. Half done ones are left out.
- **The Fielding tab** (consultant and Client Admin, after launch) shows:
  - **Response monitor:** invited, started and submitted by cohort. Cohorts under five are folded into one line, and nobody is named.
  - **Window:** the close date and any extensions. The consultant may extend once, by up to 5 working days, with a reason. A second extension needs the Super Admin. There is no third.
  - **Quality:** the median time and the flags (for example speeding), by response number only, never the answers. The consultant can void a response with a reason. The void is logged and cannot be undone.
  - **360 comments:** after freeze, the consultant reads each leader's rater comments and may redact one. The original is kept, encrypted.
- **Reminders** go out once a day by themselves: on working days 3 and 6, 24 hours before close, and a final one only to people who started but did not submit. Nobody who has submitted gets one.
- **An objection stops the links.** If a respondent objects on the rights page, their survey, 360 and pulse links stop working at once.
- **Leadership 360 forms** now have open questions (draft wording until PAC sends the v1.1 forms) and an integrity notice that each person ticks before submitting. A rater answering outside the leader's rater dates gets the "link no longer active" page.
- **Freeze** now records a summary: invited, started, submitted, abandoned, voided, auto submitted, extensions and the fingerprint.
- **New rules for new assessments (2.1).** A response counts as speeding below 40% of the median time, any two quality flags exclude it, and a 360 section needs at least three observed items. Assessments scored earlier keep their rules and give the same results.

**Section D, Targets and standards.** Section D needs Section C saved first. For each KPI the 12 month target: **13, 45, 7, 6, 75**. The 3 year target for the delivery time KPI: **6**. 2. Disagreement is kept quiet and shows up as slow delivery.
3. Recognition is for hitting the number, rarely for helping someone.
4. A few long serving supervisors hold real influence.
5. Weekly meetings are long and one way.
6. Information is held back until it is too late to act.

**Section F, Target behaviours.** Five leadership behaviours, each tagged with an orientation. They must describe something you can see someone do, not a value:

| Behaviour | Orientation |
|---|---|

| Explains the reason behind a decision in the team meeting | Engage |
| Reviews the weekly numbers with the team and agrees one action | Perform |
| Spends time on the shop floor listening to staff | Engage |

| Start: practices | A short weekly huddle across teams / One page decision rights / Online delivery confirmation |
| Keep and protect | Our food safety standards |

**Section H, Processes and enablers.** Six short texts: *Annual review with a yearly rating. / A one day induction, then on the job. / Promotion goes to the best sales or delivery numbers. / Bonuses are paid on volume only. / Approvals wait for the finance manager. / A new online ordering system next year.* Tick **H3 contradicts what happens in practice** and **H4 contradicts what happens in practice**. ## 5. What can go wrong, and what to say

| You meet | What it means | Say or do |
|---|---|---|
| "This link is no longer active" | The link was used, or the window closed, or it is unknown. All three look the same on purpose. | "It cannot show why, so a link cannot be probed." |
| "Too few people answered" | A group under five. | "This protects anonymity; the group is counted in the organisation's results." |
| Launch checks block | A named check fails, for example no consent approval or an unfinished section. | Fix what the page names. |
| Cannot freeze | The window is still open. | Wait for the close time. |
| Publishing blocked by a pre condition | A finding that needs a pre condition item first. | "The plan makes the leadership team do the groundwork first." |
| A report section says not available | The client was scored before that measure existed (before 17 October 2026). | "New assessments carry it; this one keeps the score it was frozen with." |

**Known limits to say before they are found.**

- The consent wording is a draft and needs legal review.
- Staff sign in with a password; two step sign in is not enforced yet.
- The consultant launches. The specification has the Client Admin launch after the consultant approves; this is planned.
- Reminders to people who have not answered are not sent yet; the consultant re issues links.
- The organisational report has a full version and a brief; the Board summary exists; the sub culture, leader, leadership team and action plan reports are planned.
- Report text comes from a fixed PAC wording library driven by the data, plus the consultant's own notes; AI drafting comes after the first client.
- The survey is English only.
- A survey window cannot be extended after launch.
- The scoring and reporting figures are produced by one engine that has 271 tests and matches an independent Python version byte for byte.
