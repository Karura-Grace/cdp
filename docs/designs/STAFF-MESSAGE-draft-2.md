## Version A: full anonymity (the default)

Subject: Your voice on how we work at {org}

Dear colleagues,

We are asking every one of you to take part in a short survey about how work really gets done at {org}, and how you would like it to be. I am asking you personally, because the answers will shape the decisions we make about how we lead and how we work. {purpose}

We have asked Priority Activator Consulting (PAC Africa) to run it for us. PAC is independent of our management, and it is PAC, not us, that handles your answers.

**What it asks.** The survey takes about 40 minutes. You can stop and come back to it later using the same link. There are no right or wrong answers. We want what you see and what you would like, not what you think we want to hear. If something is unclear, or the link does not work, contact {help}.

**Your answers are anonymous.** Your name and email address are never stored with your answers. The moment you submit, the link between you and your answers is destroyed and cannot be put back. Nobody at {org} and nobody at PAC can see what any one person answered. Results are shown only for groups of five or more people, so no one can be picked out of a small group.

**How the results will be used.** They will guide how we lead and how we work. They will never be used to assess, promote, reward or discipline any individual.

**Your link is yours alone.** It is personal and works only once. Please do not forward it or share it. If you think someone else has opened it, tell {help} and a new one will be issued.

**It is your choice.** Taking part is voluntary. If you do not want to be surveyed, you can tell us at any time through the "Your rights" page on the survey, and your link will stop working at once. Nothing will be held against you.

When the survey closes, PAC prepares a report of combined results. We will share what we learn with you, and tell you what we are going to do about it.

Thank you for your honesty. It is the only way this works.

{sender}
{role}, {org}
