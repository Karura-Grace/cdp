## Who reads
CEO and executives, People or HR lead, team leaders. Formats: PAC branded PDF, PowerPoint slides, on screen in the platform. Additions cover new sections or pages, new charts or visuals, layout and branding, wording and recommendations.

## Sub-culture report
- Long, 30+ pages. Opens with a headline findings page.
- Sections: group comparison page, heatmap across all groups, sub-culture verdicts (confirmed or indicative), per group recommendations (drafted by the platform, edited by the consultant).
- Cuts: division and department, location, grade and executive versus staff, tenure band.
- Groups under 5: folded into the parent, named as merged.
- Each group page: profile chart against the organisation, SDI and divergence box, readiness and eOS panel, recommendations box.
- Trend: reserve space, fill at the second cycle. (Owner first said "from the prototype data", then chose reserve space.)
- One page hand-out per confirmed sub-culture, released with the report.

## Leader report
- Two versions: short (for the debrief) and full (consultant and coach).
- Content: 360 by rater group, fit with the team culture, blind spots and strengths, development actions.
- 360 page: self versus others bar chart per orientation, blind spot and hidden strength callouts, open comments page, leadership target comparison.
- Delivered by a PAC consultant in a debrief. No comparison with other leaders.
- Carrier class: plain language wording, no class names for the leader. Consultant holds the label.
- Tone: warm and coaching.

## Board summary
- Five to eight pages. First page: culture today versus target, alignment percentage, sub-culture signals, leadership signal.

## All reports
- Next steps section drafted by the platform, edited by the consultant before release.
- Common pages: how to read this report, glossary, technical appendix, confidentiality and use notice.
